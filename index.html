A little about me

From bus trackers to brain maps

My journey started with code. As a software engineering student at UTM, I built a real-time campus bus tracker, and somewhere along the way I realised what excited me most wasn't the app, it was how people learn to build things like it.

That curiosity took me through a master's on teaching computational thinking with robots, and a PhD on using ontologies and learning analytics to understand students' competencies.

Today I'm back in the classroom (and the lab), now at UMT, where I get to do what I love every day: teach, research, and bring tech closer to communities that need it most.
