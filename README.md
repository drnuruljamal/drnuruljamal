## 👩‍💻 About Me

I am a lecturer and researcher at the Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT), Malaysia. My academic background spans software engineering, informatic engineering, computing education, learning analytics, and educational technology.

My research focuses on developing adaptive and competency-based learning frameworks, integrating artificial intelligence into education, and designing technology-enhanced learning experiences. I am particularly interested in using data-driven approaches and intelligent systems to support student learning, competency development, and evidence-based educational decision-making.

- **Current Position:** Lecturer, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT)
- **Research Interests:** Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, Computing Education, Learning Analytics, Adaptive Learning, Educational Technology, Applied Soft Computing, and Digital Transformation
- **Research Focus:** Competency Modelling, Computational Thinking, Educational Robotics, Explainable AI, and AI-Enhanced Learning
- **Community Engagement:** Educational robotics, digital competency development, and AI education outreach
- **Email:** [nurul.jamal@umt.edu.my](mailto:nurul.jamal@umt.edu.my)

## 🚀 Research Projects

### Projek Komuniti Nelayan Pintar

**Project Leader | 2026**

*Pemerkasaan Kompetensi Digital, Pemikiran Komputasional (CT), dan Aplikasi AI Generatif Murid B40 Luar Bandar*

Supported by the Grant of Environmental and Social Sustainability Funds, 2026.

The project focuses on empowering rural and B40 secondary school students through digital competency development, computational thinking, and responsible use of generative AI.

### An Ontology-Driven Learning Analytics Framework

Research on ontology-driven learning analytics to diagnose and predict student competencies, connecting learning outcomes with competency indicators to support evidence-based educational decision-making.

### Adaptive Learning through Educational Robotics

Research on adaptive learning frameworks that integrate educational robotics to develop computational thinking and problem-solving skills among students.
